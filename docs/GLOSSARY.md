# Italian translation: wording choices

The terms, names and phrasings chosen for `edits_it/`, and why. Keep new translations consistent
with this list, and update it when a choice changes. (Builds and the GUI read only the `*.txt` files
here, so this file is never built into the disc.)

## Forms of address

* **Odin is addressed formally:** *voi*, "mio signore", "al vostro servizio".
* **Valkyrie, Freya and Frei use *tu*** with each other.
* **Platina addresses Madre Laia's guest formally:** "I vostri vestiti…", "Perdonatemi…".
* **Platina and Lucian call her parents *mamma* and *papà*:** "Mother and Father" becomes "Mamma e papà".
* **Arngrim's chapter:** brothers, mercenaries, Lombert to Arngrim and Valkyrie to humans use *tu*.
  Subjects address royalty with *voi* (Lombert to Jelanda, Jelanda's spirit to Valkyrie). Arngrim gives
  Angela an ironic *voi* until she is unmasked, then calls her *piccola* with *tu*. He mocks the King
  with *voi*. Jelanda uses *tu* downward (waitress, Lombert, Arngrim), and Lawfer uses *voi* with his
  father, the Knight Captain.
* **Belenus's storyline:** Asaka and Maria give Belenus *voi* ("signor Belenus", "signorino"); he
  uses *tu* with them and *voi* with his father, and *voi* with Valkyrie, a stranger. Valkyrie uses *tu*.
  Jayle gives her captain Fahn *voi* and slips into *tu* when she forgets herself ("Stupido!").
* **Crell Monferaigne:** Bert gives his superior Ernest *voi*; Lezard's letter gives his old teacher Lorenta
  *voi*; the priest prays to the Battle-Maiden with *tu*, as in a prayer. Llewelyn and Millia use *tu*;
  Millia's "Perché non volete capire?!" is plural, to both parents. Genevieve and Magnus use *tu*.
* **The gods:** everyone gives Odin *voi* ("Sommo Odino, fermatevi!!") and he uses *tu*. Freya uses *tu*
  with Valkyrie, Frei and Loki. Loki uses *tu* with Valkyrie, Freya and Surt, and with Odin once he
  defies him openly (scene 0x19). Valkyrie uses *tu* with everyone except Odin.
* **Lezard** gives Valkyrie and his old teacher Lorenta a courtly *voi*; he uses *tu* with Mystina
  ("Mysty"), Lucian, Arngrim, Bellion and the elf. Mystina uses *tu* with everyone, sarcastically;
  Arngrim too. Servants and students give their master *voi* ("maestro Lezard").
* **Strangers:** adults meeting for the first time use *voi* (Dolce, Lyseria, Grey and Gandar to
  Valkyrie); Valkyrie answers with *tu*. Exceptions by character: Aelia and Badrach use *tu*
  with Valkyrie from the start.
* **Nanami** gives her foster parents and Valkyrie *voi*; Lucian gives Dolce *voi* ("signora Dolce").
* **Badrach speaks colloquially** ("un sacco", "chi se ne frega", "Ci si vede!"), without dialect spellings.

## Item names

The rule follows the official Italian release of Final Fantasy VIII, which translated its potions and
materials and kept every weapon name in English (Final Fantasy IX mixed the two). The full list is in
`battle_1806.1.txt`, messages `0x7D1`-`0xA34`; a name listed unchanged stays English on purpose.

* **Italian:** consumables, materials, gems and crystals, accessories, armour, artifacts and quest
  items. A proper name inside one stays: Perla di Karula, Arpa di Atrasia, Insegnamenti di Bethanus.
* **English:** every weapon, generic ones too (Long Sword, Short Bow, Crossbow), and the named swords,
  spears, bows and wands; Freya's and Brahms' named gear (Dragoon Faith, Ether Freeze, Eternal Shine,
  Brisingal, Ether Laser, Bloody-Knuckle, Bloody-Duster); the spell scrolls, whose names are the
  spells'. A skill book carries its skill's name (see Skills and abilities).
* **Dialogue follows the menus:** "Hai trovato Long Sword." but "Hai trovato Occhio del Cielo.".

## Skills and abilities

The item rule carried over: a skill that names what it does is translated, a named technique is not.

* **Italian:** the utility and stat skills, the traits, every help line and move description, and the
  Skill and Ability screens.
* **English:** the techniques (Adept Illusion, Dancing Sword, Last Trial, Splash, Reverie, Noise Arrow,
  Triple Distress, Scarlet Edge, Charge, Strike Edge, Dark, Trick Step, Slanting Rain, Darkness Arrow,
  False Arrow), every attack in the Ability menu (Bolt Slash, High Wind, Nibelung Valesti), the spells
  and Great Magic. The mages' "Magic" entry among the battle attacks is "Magia".
* **Skill books** carry their skill's name, so 17 of them are Italian now ("Hai trovato Grinta.").

## Conventions kept from the English text

* The 「 opening bracket on spoken lines, and the game's ・・・ and ... ellipses as they were.
* Laughs are Italian: "He he he" → "Eh eh eh", "Hee hee hee" → "Hi hi hi".
* Every control code (`<3:..>` typing delays, `<12:..>` voice clips, `<17:..>` waits, `<18>`,
  `<19:..>`, `<7:..>` widths) stays where it was relative to the words it times.
* A line with no words (only ・・・) is left out of the edit file, so it stays byte for byte as it was.
* A line that stays in English on purpose is listed in its edit file unchanged, so `vp_progress.py`
  counts it as done. `build` leaves such an entry byte for byte as it was. This covers:
  * names only, such as Platina's 「Lucian?」 and 「Lucian...」;
  * the character and place-name lists (menu 6, linked to the camp menus; battle 1500.9 0x01-0x19);
  * speaker labels in the battle hints (1988.2);
  * DME, Stereo, ON and OFF.
  * "Scenario Error 1" (system record 12, a debug message).
  * The debug menu, scene 0x0 (Japanese developer text in raw glyph codes).
  * The battle debug menu in 1500.7: `0x10` ("Debug function"), `0x28`-`0x36`, `0x50`-`0x66` and
    `0x82`-`0xA1` ("Hit Data Display", "Dme Change", "Enemy Annihilate", the sound test). Players
    never see it, and translating it would only take room in the battle font.
  * The skills `0x405`-`0x419` in 1500.7 that stay English as named techniques (Adept Illusion,
    Dancing Sword …).

  Still left out, so they count as not done: LV and CT, and "WeaknessWeak" (1500.7 `0x1A`). That one
  looks like two words packed into one string, and the code that splits it isn't known yet.
